Take a closer look.

Teal silicone, cardinal piping and a translucent tap dome. Nothing personal is stored on the band.

Battery
72 hours

Target on one charge. Magnetic two-pad charging in about 40 minutes.

Memory
500 taps

Saved as band ID, time and signal. Survives a power cycle.

Weight
28 g

210 × 34 mm. 3.2 mm thick, 6.2 mm at the dome.

Fit
13–19 cm

10 holes at 7 mm pitch. Stainless snap stud.

Identity
Random ID

8 characters. QR code and serial on the lining.

Splash proof. 10,000 bends at 20 mm radius. Survives a 1.5 m drop. Latex free. Charging stops above 45 °C.

Private by default.

Your profile is opt in, and you decide what each person sees.

Contact details need both of you.

People you tap see your name, school, project and tags. Discord or LinkedIn appear only if you both switch them on.

Your notes stay yours.

Reasons and notes are private. Nobody else can see them.

No location tracking.

The band records who and when, never where.

Gone 90 days after the event.

Quiet mode, block, report, remove and delete are always in the app.

Reports go to organizers under the TreeHacks Code of Conduct.
